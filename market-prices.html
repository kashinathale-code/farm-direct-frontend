Why Compare with Mandi Modal Rates?

The Modal Price is the single most frequent transaction rate recorded in an APMC mandi on a given day. Traditional mandi sales involve a 6-10% middleman commission, mandi taxes, weighing fees, and transport markups that reduce the farmer's net earning. On Farm Direct, farmers sell directly to buyers at transparent prices benchmarked to these rates, earning higher net margins while buyers pay less than urban retail rates.
