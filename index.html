How Farm Direct Works

Eliminating multi-tiered brokers to return full market value to hardworking growers.

01

Farmer Publishes Harvest

Farmers list produce with authentic photos, harvest dates, quantities, and per-kg rates. They can optionally enable live price negotiation or bulk group-buying campaigns.

02

Buyer Discovers & Connects

Buyers explore verified crop listings, scan QR codes for tamper-resistant traceability certificates, and place direct orders or negotiate custom bulk counteroffers.

03

Direct Fulfillment & Tracking

Orders are confirmed with live timeline tracking. Real-time buyer-farmer direct messaging ensures complete transparency from field to fork.

Technology for Agriculture

Engineered for Agricultural Fairness

Modern e-commerce tools built specifically to protect and empower crop growers.

🤝

Real-Time B2B Price Negotiation

Farmers set optional reserve prices while buyers can negotiate bulk rates with validated counteroffers and automatic order conversion upon mutual agreement.

👥

Community Group Buying

Buyers pool orders together to hit quantity targets and unlock wholesale discounts, bringing co-op purchasing power to everyday consumers.

🛡️

QR Crop Traceability

Every harvest produces a scannable QR code linking to a tamper-resistant public traceability certificate validating farm origin and harvest freshness.

🤖

AI Marketplace Assistant

Our intelligent assistant answers crop pricing questions, recommends produce within your budget, and tracks your order status 24/7.

📊

Farmer Sales Analytics

Growers track delivered revenue, order fulfillment stages, incoming buyer proposals, and automated low-stock warnings.

🔔

Persistent Notifications

Never miss a purchase order, status update, counteroffer, or message with real-time in-app notification indicators.
