How Farm Direct Works

Cutting out multi-tiered brokers to return full market value to growers.

01

Farmer Lists Harvest

Farmers list fresh crops with high-res photos, harvest dates, quantities, and per-kg rates. They can optionally enable live price negotiation or bulk group-buying.

02

Buyer Discovers & Connects

Buyers browse verified crop listings, scan QR codes for harvest traceability, and place orders directly or submit negotiation counteroffers.

03

Direct Delivery & Fulfillment

Orders are confirmed with live timeline tracking. Real-time buyer-farmer messaging ensures total transparency from field to fork.

Real-Time Bidding & Offers

Farmers set optional reserve prices while buyers can negotiate bulk rates with validated counteroffers and automatic order conversion.

Community Group Buying

Buyers pool orders together to hit quantity targets and unlock wholesale discounts, bringing co-op purchasing power to everyone.

The Direct-to-Consumer Difference

Traditional agricultural supply chains pass produce through 4–6 intermediaries (village agents, commission brokers, wholesale APMC mandis, secondary distributors, retail shops), each extracting a margin while adding transit delay.

Traditional Mandi System: Farmer gets 35% - 45%
Farm Direct Platform: Farmer gets 95% - 100%
